POMO – The Personal Healthcare Companion

Problem Statement

🏥 Affordable continuous monitoring and emergency response for people with episodic conditions
😷 Stroke risk, migraines, asthma, panic attacks
📍 Help is often not nearby, especially in rural India
🚫 No affordable monitoring, emotional support or data control; existing tools are app/web only

Proposed Solution

⌚ Wrist wearable + 🤖 tabletop robot
🔍 Screens for risk and warns early
🗣️ Runs a spoken stroke (FAST) check
⏰ Reminds medicines and calls for help

Innovation & Uniqueness

🤖 Physical companion: speaks, listens, shows emotion, runs a voice stroke check
📶 Works offline: BLE link and on-device checks, runs through power cuts and poor internet
💸 Affordable: low-cost open-source parts, about ₹4,500–6,500 in total
🔐 Patient-owned data: encrypted, consent-based sharing, ABDM-aligned

How It Addresses the Problem

🩺 Risk screening: stroke FAST voice check with fall and pulse alerts; migraine trigger and early-symptom tracking
⏰ Personalised voice medication reminders
❤️ Emotional support via animated eyes and a warm voice
🌍 Multilingual voice for elders and non-technical users
🚨 Emergency escalation: stroke red flags send SMS/calls to family or hospital at once
🔒 Encrypted storage; sharing only with the patient's permission

Flow

1️⃣ Sense vitals, motion and triggers
2️⃣ BLE transfer to the robot
3️⃣ Rule-based risk check
4️⃣ Stroke: spoken FAST check → instant SMS/call with onset time
5️⃣ Migraine: voice warning, medicine reminder, log → escalate if worse
6️⃣ Daily care

Technical Approach

📡 Sensing: MAX30102 (heart rate/SpO2), MPU6050 (motion), skin temperature; robot adds mic, BME280 and light sensing
🧠 Analysis: ESP32 runs rule-based checks on separate stroke and migraine paths
🚑 Escalation: failed FAST check or no reply → 4G modem sends SMS/calls
🔊 Care: DFPlayer Mini voice reminders, symptom log, animated OLED eyes
🔋 Power: 3.3 V deep-sleep Li-Po wearable; 5 V robot with capacitor buffer for modem spikes

Business Idea

💡 An affordable healthcare companion (about ₹4,500–6,500 prototype cost) that moves care from reactive to proactive, continuous monitoring
🏥 Market: hospital home-care programmes (Apollo, Fortis), MoHFW/ABDM-aligned efforts, NGOs and rural health programmes
💰 Revenue: device sales; hospital, care-centre and NGO bundles; optional consent-based caregiver/doctor sync
📊 Pilot metrics: missed-dose reduction, false-alert rate, onset-to-alert time, FAST-check completion rate

Impact & Benefits

😌 Daily emotional support reduces anxiety and loneliness
🏡 Elderly and chronic patients can live safely at home
💊 Consistent tracking aims to cut missed doses
⚠️ Faster stroke alerts and early warnings aim to cut delays and hospital visits
💰 Lower medical costs, less burden on families
🔐 Patients control their own records, in line with ABDM

POMO – The Personal Healthcare Companion

Challenge Statement

😷 Millions with episodic conditions (epilepsy, asthma, migraines, heart issues) lack affordable monitoring & timely intervention
⚡ Need real-time vital monitoring + emergency alerts
🗣️ Require multilingual guidance, emotional support, medication reminders
🔒 Need secure health data that stays under the patient's control

Technical Highlights

🛠️ Dual design: Wrist wearable + tabletop robot
⚙️ Core: 2x ESP32, MAX30102 sensor, SSD1306 OLED eyes
🔊 Audio: DFPlayer Mini + speaker
📡 Connectivity: SIM800L GSM for emergencies
🔋 Features: AI safety checks, encrypted data, ABDM alignment

Feasibility & Viability

✅ Technically achievable with available sensors/AI
🏠 Practical for home use, minimal effort
⏱️ Strong need: Seconds count in sudden episodes
📱 Easy adoption: Simple interface
🌐 Supported by MoHFW, ABDM, WHO, UNICEF

Research Status

🧠 Stroke: research about half done
🔬 Migraine: study is ongoing
🩺 Starts with one disease first, then the model will be trained to work for multiple diseases
✅ Working now: vital monitoring, alerts, reminders, emotional support, emergency escalation
🚧 Stroke and migraine prediction are under research, not finished features

Proposed Solution

🤖 POMO: Robot companion for episodic conditions & rural India access
⌚ Wrist wearable tracks vital signs
🗣️ Tabletop robot talks, listens, offers emotional support
🚨 Handles emergencies, medication reminders, encrypted data & AI safety

How It Helps?

🔍 Early detection of warning signs
⏰ Personalized medication reminders
❤️ Emotional support via expressive eyes & warm voice
🌍 Multilingual interface for elders/non-tech users
👫 Friendly companionship & encouragement

Impact & Benefits

😌 Reduces anxiety/loneliness & episodes
🏡 Enables independence for elderly/chronic patients
⚠️ Early warnings prevent hospital visits
💰 Lower medical costs
🔐 Patient data stays encrypted and under patient control (ABDM-aligned)

Competitor Analysis & Cost

🏆 POMO edges: Physical robot, patient data control, robot + web access
⚔️ Beats Babylon, Philips, MedVault, Apple Health in key features
🖥️ Interface: Robot + Web
💸 Prototype cost: ₹2,500–2,900.
